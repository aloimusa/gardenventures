# Wind down audio attribution

Source attribution is retained here rather than in the timer interface.

- **White Noise and Heartbeat** by **canton** is included unchanged as a local MP3, retrieved from [Abitti's background-audio collection](https://cheat.abitti.fi/build/index.html?lang=fi&tab=muzak). The [original SoundCloud page](https://soundcloud.com/canton/white-noise-and-heartbeat) identifies the license as **CC BY**, but does not specify a version; Garden therefore describes it only as **CC BY (license version not specified)**.
- **Soft static, Rain and Stream** are synthesized placeholders generated locally for Garden from deterministic noise and filters. They are not field recordings and contain no third-party recordings or samples.

Download URLs, technical details, license evidence, recipes and checksums are recorded in [the audio source ledger](../public/audio/SOURCES.md).
